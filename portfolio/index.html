Profile

Renewable energy, funded and built

Over ten years in the energy sector, working across renewable energy investments — securing European funding and project finance, structuring the investment case, and carrying projects through to a working asset. Battery storage is where that work is sharpest.

My work covers the full investment lifecycle — identifying the opportunity, shaping the investment strategy, testing feasibility, securing financing and funding, running procurement, and carrying the project through implementation to delivery.

Most of that work sits where three disciplines meet: how renewable and storage assets are engineered, the financial structure that makes them viable, and the funding and regulatory frameworks that decide whether they get built at all. A project that is technically sound but unfunded is not a project. I coordinate energy investments end to end, including utility-scale developments delivered under confidentiality.

Engineering, design and permitting are delivered by specialist partners I select and coordinate. My work is the investment case, the funding and the execution that holds them together — and the responsibility for the result.
